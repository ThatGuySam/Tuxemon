## Proof Boundary

The gate covers concrete declared paths and input cases, not every possible history. Host delay/error fixtures are explicitly synthetic and are separate from real model/HTTP evidence. Emulator success does not establish physical display behavior. Large scene changes may update several frames while retaining content. The full world quest route does not claim all 30 reply outcomes or dedicated wall/water/NPC attempted-entry tests.

Use `/maintain-verification-skill` when screens or entry points change. The feature map and the enforced coverage manifest must change together.
